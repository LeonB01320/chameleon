# Manchester technology recruitment leadership longlist

Checked through Leon's LinkedIn Recruiter account on 5 November 2026. 23 stronger profile-backed matches, plus 6 additional profiles with broader remits or details to confirm. This is a sourcing longlist, not a statement of interest in moving jobs.

## Method and limits

- LinkedIn Recruiter's postal-radius filter: within 10 miles (16 km) of M2 5GU, central Manchester. Every listed candidate was returned by that filter. Broad profile labels such as Manchester Area or Greater Manchester are reproduced below; these do not independently verify an exact home/work address or distance. The centre is a practical central-Manchester postcode, not a formally supplied city boundary.
- Current-title search included team lead, team leader, recruitment manager, managing consultant, business manager, delivery manager and divisional manager, combined with technology-recruitment phrases. Equivalent management titles were screened manually. Managing Consultant is included as a title-level match; direct reports are not established for every candidate.
- The resulting search showed 136 results. Reviewed 129 distinct visible result cards across its six pages and opened 32 selected profiles. This is a substantial longlist, not an exhaustive market map.
- Checked current-role start months against the 18-month requirement as of 5 November 2026, rather than using company tenure or rounded LinkedIn duration badges. All main-list role dates are April 2025 or earlier. A May 2025 entry would require day-level confirmation at this cutoff.
- Technology specialism was supported by the profile headline, summary or role description. Included software, data, cyber, infrastructure, ERP, embedded systems and technology transformation. The main list labels in-house recruiters separately from agency/service-provider recruiters.
- Profile information is self-reported and may be stale. Listed roles were marked Present when inspected. Overlapping entries, employer discrepancies and broader remits are flagged rather than silently resolved. No outreach, project saves or candidate contact occurred.
